<objective>
Eliminate the paper-trading regression where POST `/api/trades` and `/api/trades/system` throw an "Unexpected error" by ensuring each endpoint eagerly loads the `Trade.orders` relationship before passing the new trade into `_serialize_trade`.

Purpose: UAT discovered that `_serialize_trade` iterates `trade.orders`, but the POST endpoints never refresh that relationship after commit. We need a durable fix that keeps the relationship populated so serialization never tries to lazy-load from a detached session.

Output: Both POST handlers reload the newly created trade with its related orders using `AsyncSession.refresh(..., attribute_names=["orders"])`, guaranteeing serialization succeeds even immediately after commit.
</objective>
